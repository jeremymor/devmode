## The Brief

Build your first website and deploy it to the internet. This is the moment your code goes from "running on my laptop" to "anyone in the world can see it."

You'll create a multi-page site with a working contact form. When someone fills out the form, it should actually do something — send you an email, save to a database, or post to a Slack channel. You'll learn what a server actually does and how to deploy your work live.
